Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Avail Technologies collects business contact information (name, business email, phone number, company name) submitted through consultation requests and contact inquiries. For operational telemetry and reporting services, aggregate transaction counts and device health metrics are processed to ensure synchronization reliability.

2. Store & Transaction Data Ownership

All retail, financial, inventory, and customer data processed across your software systems belongs solely to your organization. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your business transaction data. Local databases remain encrypted on your branch appliances.

3. Data Security

All cloud communications between store LAN appliances and cloud services use TLS 1.3 encryption. Cloud tenant databases are partitioned with strict role-based access control and continuous automated backups.

4. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions about our data practices or wish to request data erasure, contact us at privacy@availtechnologies.com.
